The salon

A dream that became a Frankfurt fixture.

With his own hair salon, owner Abdul Boushaba fulfilled a dream. What began on Albanusstraße in Frankfurt-Höchst now has three homes – two in Höchst and one in Bockenheim.

The atmosphere is relaxed and family-like, with a true barbershop feel. Whether it’s a quick trim, a clean fade, a shave or styling for a special occasion – we take time for what you want.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Thanks to Cut & Go you can simply walk in – with or without an appointment. If you prefer to plan ahead, just give your salon a call.

How much is a men’s haircut?

A “dry” haircut is €15; the “complete” haircut with washing, blow-drying and styling starts at €15. You’ll find all prices under Services & prices.

When is kids’ day?

Every Tuesday. Haircuts for children up to 10 years old are just €7.

How does the bonus card work?

Ask for your bonus card in the salon: after 10 visits, your next haircut is free.

Where can I find Boushaba?

Twice in Frankfurt-Höchst (Albanusstraße 31 and Emmerich-Josef-Straße 7) and once in Bockenheim (Adalbertstraße 25).

What are your opening hours?

Our salons are generally open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 8 pm and on Saturdays from 9 am to 7 pm. You’ll find the exact hours for each salon under Locations.

Can I buy hair care products?

Yes. We work exclusively with professional products, many of which you can buy right in the salon.

Ready for a fresh cut?

Drop by or call your salon – we look forward to seeing you.
